# World Population Analytics – Summary

## 📌 Objective
Analyze global population data using SQL Server to understand population distribution, growth trends, and regional contributions across countries and continents.

---

## 🌍 Population Distribution Insights
- Asia contributes the largest share of the global population.  
- A small number of countries account for a significant portion of the world’s population.  
- Population distribution is uneven across continents.

---

## 📈 Growth Trends
- Countries such as India and Nigeria show strong population growth between 2015 and 2022.  
- Some regions exhibit slower growth, indicating demographic stabilization.  
- Growth rate analysis highlights rapidly expanding populations.

---

## 🛠 SQL Techniques Used
- Aggregations and grouping  
- Self-joins for year-wise comparison  
- Window functions (RANK, running totals, percentile)  
- CTEs for ranking and filtering  
- Subqueries for global and regional comparisons  

---

## 📊 Business & Policy Value
- Supports demographic and economic planning  
- Helps governments allocate resources effectively  
- Assists researchers in understanding long-term population trends  
- Enables comparative regional analysis
